# Workflow: Professional Visual & Design Review

Conduct rigorous design audits distinguishing objective defects from subjective preferences.

## Audit Dimensions

1. **Visual Hierarchy:** Can the primary heading, core message, and primary CTA be identified in 3 seconds?
2. **Optical Typography:** Is line length between 45–75 characters? Is display tracking tight (-0.02em)?
3. **Anti-Slop Scan:** Are there single-word neon highlights, arbitrary numbered badges, or generic slogan headings?
4. **Density & Alignment:** Do cards have consistent internal padding? Are baselines aligned?
5. **Accessibility & Contrast:** Do all text pairs exceed 4.5:1 contrast? Are focus rings visible?
