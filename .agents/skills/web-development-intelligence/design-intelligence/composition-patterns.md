# Composition Patterns & Layout Architectures

Move beyond predictable, centered single-column templates by employing deliberate compositional architectures.

## Layout Archetypes

1. **Editorial Asymmetry:**
   - Off-center headlines paired with large expressive typography, generous negative space, and offset visual columns.
   - Ideal for: Architecture, luxury goods, fashion, portfolio, boutique hospitality.
   - Rule: One strong horizontal anchor line must ground the asymmetrical layout so it feels intentional, not broken.

2. **The Bento Grid (When Justified):**
   - Modular grid layout containing tiles of varied aspect ratios (1x1, 2x1, 1x2, 2x2) showcasing distinct facets of a product.
   - **Condition for Use:** Content must represent diverse, non-hierarchical, multi-media facets (e.g. video preview, stat counter, live interactive widget, short quote).
   - **Anti-Pattern:** Do not force uniform, identical feature lists into a faux bento grid.

3. **Split-Screen Dual Canvas:**
   - Viewport divided into 50/50 or 60/40 vertical columns. One side remains sticky (e.g. value proposition and CTA) while the other side scrolls through detailed interactive cards or case studies.
   - Ideal for: SaaS product showcases, complex service explanations, interactive configuration tools.

4. **Layered Overlapping Horizons:**
   - Elements (cards, typography, photographic cutouts) deliberately overlap across background section transitions with z-index layering.
   - Creates depth and breaks the rigid horizontal "striped section" feel typical of generic sites.
