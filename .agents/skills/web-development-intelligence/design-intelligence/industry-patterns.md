# Industry UX Patterns & Commercial Playbooks

Specific structural architectures for key commercial sectors.

## Sector Blueprints

### 1. Restaurants & Hospitality
- **User Intent:** Check menu, see atmosphere, book table, find address/hours, order delivery.
- **Section Flow:**
  1. Hero: Atmosphere imagery, cuisine specialty, reservation CTA & opening hours.
  2. Curated Menu Highlights: Starters, Mains, Signature Cocktails with dietary tags (VG, GF).
  3. Atmosphere & Interior: Cinematic gallery or video loop.
  4. Chef / Story: Short origin story establishing craft.
  5. Direct Booking Widget: Date, time, party size picker.
  6. Practical Details: Google Map embed, parking, transport, hours, contact.

### 2. Real Estate (Agency Without Pre-Built Catalog)
- **User Intent:** Request valuation, hire selling agent, discuss property search.
- **Section Flow (Lead-Gen Focus):**
  1. Hero: Trusted positioning ("Selling prime residential property in [City]"), prominent valuation CTA.
  2. Proven Track Record: Total sales volume, average days on market, recent sales ticker.
  3. Methodology / The Selling Process: 4-step clear explanation of how the agency works.
  4. Neighborhood Expertise: Focus areas, local price guides.
  5. Valuation Inquiry Form: Address, property type, timeline, contact details.
  6. Client Video Testimonials & Licensing Credentials.

### 3. Tourism & Destination Travel
- **User Intent:** Get inspired, explore itineraries, check dates, book tour.
- **Section Flow:**
  1. Hero: Fullscreen cinematic video/image, destination headline, trip search bar.
  2. Curated Expeditions / Packages: Cards with duration, group size, physical rating, price.
  3. Interactive Itinerary: Day-by-day scrollytelling with pinned maps and photography.
  4. Included vs Excluded: Clear transparent checklist (flights, meals, guides).
  5. Traveler Reviews & Instagram/Community feed.
  6. Inquire / Reserve: Booking flow with deposit information.

### 4. E-Commerce & Retail
- **User Intent:** Find item, check price, verify quality/reviews, purchase securely.
- **Section Flow:**
  1. Hero: Seasonal collection or flagship product launch with direct "Shop now".
  2. Category Quick-Nav: Visual circular or square tiles for top categories.
  3. Best Sellers / Featured Grid: Clear pricing, color swatch preview, quick add-to-cart.
  4. Brand Value Pillars: Free shipping thresholds, sustainable materials, 30-day returns.
  5. Customer Reviews with user photos.
  6. Sticky Mobile Add-to-Cart Bar on product details.

### 5. eSIM & High-Velocity Digital Utilities
- **User Intent:** Choose country, compare data plans, buy and install in 2 minutes.
- **Section Flow:**
  1. Hero: Instant country/region search input ("Where are you traveling?").
  2. Popular Destination Cards (Japan, USA, Europe, Turkey) with starting prices.
  3. Plan Comparison: Data amount (1GB, 5GB, 10GB, Unlimited), validity days, hotspot support.
  4. 3-Step Setup Guide: Scan QR code → Install profile → Turn on roaming.
  5. Device Compatibility Checker: Dropdown to verify iPhone/Android model.
  6. FAQ: What if I run out of data? Can I keep my WhatsApp number?

### 6. Corporate & B2B Professional Services
- **User Intent:** Verify credibility, understand enterprise capabilities, request RFP.
- **Section Flow:**
  1. Hero: Precise enterprise value proposition, client logo ticker.
  2. Core Capabilities: Detailed service pillars with measurable case studies.
  3. Executive Leadership & Security/Compliance Certifications (SOC2, ISO).
  4. Thought Leadership / Whitepapers.
  5. Contact / RFP Submission Form with direct calendar booking option.
