# Case Study: High-Conversion E-Commerce & Retail

Architecture for frictionless e-commerce browsing and checkout.

## Business Context & User Intent

- **Primary Conversion Action:** Add to cart, completed checkout.
- **Core Friction Point:** Hidden shipping fees, slow multi-step checkout, poor product imagery, lack of sizing clarity.

## Section Sequence & Architectural Blueprint

1. **Hero Campaign Banner:**
   - High-impact lifestyle image with direct collection callout ("Autumn Wool Outerwear").
   - Explicit shipping threshold badge ("Free carbon-neutral shipping over $100").

2. **Visual Category Navigation:**
   - Quick-access circular or square image cards linking directly to top departments (Coats, Knitwear, Accessories).

3. **Bestsellers & Featured Product Grid:**
   - 4-column desktop / 2-column mobile grid.
   - Product cards: primary image, subtle hover swap to lifestyle shot, color swatch preview, price, customer rating.
   - Instant "Quick Add" drawer to select size without leaving page.

4. **Brand Value Pillars:**
   - 3-column trust banner: Ethically Sourced, 30-Day Free Returns, 2-Year Warranty.

5. **Customer Photo Reviews:**
   - User-generated content gallery with verified buyer badges.
