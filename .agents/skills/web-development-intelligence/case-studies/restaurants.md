# Case Study: Modern Hospitality & Restaurant Digital Experience

A deep analysis of high-converting culinary and restaurant websites.

## Core UX Axiom: Menu-First Architecture

The vast majority of mobile and desktop restaurant visitors are looking for exactly two things within 5 seconds: **What can I eat?** and **How do I book?**
- **Menu Accessibility:** The menu must be rendered as native semantic HTML with clear typographic categorization (Starters, Mains, Grill, Desserts, Wine & Cocktails). Never force users to download a PDF file or open a zoomable JPG image.
- **Dietary & Allergen Filtering:** Clear visual tags for Vegan (VG), Vegetarian (V), Gluten-Free (GF), Dairy-Free (DF), and Nut-Free.
- **Pricing Clarity:** Tabular or clean right-aligned pricing without awkward dot-leaders.

## Photography & Ambience Guidelines

Hospitality is an emotional, sensory industry. Visual assets make or break conversion:
- **Atmospheric Hero (100vh):** High-resolution, warm-lit editorial photography showing the dining room atmosphere, open kitchen, or signature dishes.
- **Editorial Cropping:** Avoid sterile stock photos of isolated salad bowls. Use human-centric, textured photography showing glassware, warm ambient lighting, candle reflections, and craft preparation.
- **Interior & Private Dining Salon:** Dedicated asymmetrical 3-column photo grid highlighting the main room, intimate cocktail lounge, and private event spaces.

## Reservation Funnel Engineering

- **Primary Action:** Direct, prominent reservation trigger: `[Reserve a Table]`.
- **Integrated Booking Widget:** Seamless integration with platforms like OpenTable, Resy, SevenRooms, or a direct custom booking dialog.
- **Key Inputs:** Date picker, service time slot (Dinner / Lunch), party size (guests), and special request notes (allergies, anniversary).
- **Service Hours Live Badge:** Real-time status indicator on the hero ("Open today: 17:00 – 23:00").

## Section Sequence Blueprint

1. **Atmosphere Hero:** High-impact visual + Headline + Dual CTA (`[Reserve a Table]` & `[View Seasonal Menu]`) + Live Hours badge.
2. **Seasonal Menu Highlights:** Native interactive tabs with dietary filters.
3. **Space & Interior Gallery:** Editorial asymmetrical photo grid showing dining room and bar.
4. **Chef Philosophy & Farm Provenance:** Short editorial paragraph highlighting local sourcing, sustainability, and kitchen team.
5. **Private Dining & Event Enquiries:** Lead-generation form for corporate dinners, wedding receptions, and wine tastings.
6. **Location, Transit, Dress Code & Contact:** Map embed, nearest metro station/valet parking, direct tap-to-call phone number, and dress code guidance.
