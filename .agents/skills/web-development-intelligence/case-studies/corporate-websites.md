# Case Study: Corporate Enterprise & Professional Services

Architecture for high-stakes enterprise credibility and client qualification.

## Business Context & User Intent

- **Primary Conversion Action:** Schedule executive consultation, download whitepaper, initiate vendor evaluation.
- **Core Friction Point:** Buzzword soup, lack of concrete case studies, anonymous leadership.

## Section Sequence & Architectural Blueprint

1. **Strategic Value Proposition Hero:**
   - Precise statement of business impact: "Capital advisory for high-growth climate infrastructure."
   - Client logo bar featuring Fortune 500 or recognized industry partners.

2. **Practice Areas / Capability Pillars:**
   - 3–4 detailed service columns with real project summaries and deal values.

3. **Verifiable Impact Case Studies:**
   - Problem → Solution → Quantifiable Result metrics cards.

4. **Leadership & Governance:**
   - Partner bios with verified track records, previous executive roles, and direct LinkedIn links.

5. **Security, Compliance & Certifications:**
   - Prominent badges: SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, GDPR compliant.
