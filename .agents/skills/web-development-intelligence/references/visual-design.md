# Visual Design, Art Direction & Aesthetics

Create distinctive, emotionally resonant visual identities tailored to each specific brand and brief.

## Aesthetic Archetypes & Art Direction

1. **Subject-Grounded Aesthetic Selection:**
   - **Technical / Developer / Data:** Monospace accents, high-contrast borders, dark slate foundations, subtle grid/ruler accents, dense information architecture, high-efficiency data density.
   - **Luxury / Editorial / Hospitality:** Serif display faces, generous negative space, warm neutral tones, asymmetrical editorial layouts, cinematic imagery, restrained motion.
   - **Consumer Mobile / Modern SaaS:** Crisp geometric sans-serif, vibrant semantic accents, soft shadows, rounded surfaces, tactile micro-interactions, clean cards.
   - **B2B Industrial / Enterprise:** Neutral corporate palettes (navy, slate, white), high contrast, clear data tables, prominent compliance certifications, structured grids.

2. **Photography & Asset Direction:**
   - **Authentic Subject Imagery:** Use real context-specific assets. Avoid generic corporate stock photos (e.g. diverse team laughing at blank laptop).
   - **Aspect Ratio Consistency:** Maintain consistent image ratios (e.g. 16:9 for heroes, 4:3 for product cards, 1:1 for avatars) across collections.
   - **Focal Point & Object-Fit:** Use `object-fit: cover` with explicit `object-position` to ensure focal subjects remain centered across mobile and desktop crop boundaries.
