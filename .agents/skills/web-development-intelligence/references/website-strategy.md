# Website & Business Strategy

Align every visual, structural, and technical decision with the commercial reality of the project.

## Core Strategic Dimensions

1. **Transaction & Business Model Classification:**
   - **Lead Generation (High Touch / Custom Quote):** Focus on trust, qualification, pain-point resonance, proof of competence, and low-friction inquiry paths. Do not invent an e-commerce cart or empty catalog for bespoke services.
   - **Self-Serve E-Commerce / SaaS:** Prioritize product clarity, friction-free checkout, immediate price transparency, self-onboarding, and risk reversal (money-back guarantee, free trial).
   - **Local Business & Hospitality (Restaurants, Salons, Medical):** Immediate access to practical facts: location, hours, menu/services, direct booking, phone number, and atmosphere imagery.
   - **B2B Wholesale / Manufacturing:** Technical specifications, volume pricing inquiries (RFQ), compliance certifications, CAD/data sheets, and account representative routing.
   - **High-Velocity Digital Products (eSIM, Tickets, Top-ups):** Country/device selector, instant checkout, QR code provisioning instructions, and live status dashboards.

2. **Audience Intent & Decision Drivers:**
   - **Time-Constrained Decision Makers:** Demand immediate value proposition in the hero, scannable bullet points, executive summaries, and clear next steps.
   - **Technical Evaluators:** Need architecture diagrams, security certifications, API documentation, and side-by-side comparison tables.
   - **Emotional / Lifestyle Buyers:** Driven by high-fidelity photography, brand narrative, social alignment, and sensory aesthetics.

3. **Conversion Funnel Architecture:**
   - **Single Primary Conversion Action:** Every page must have one unambiguous primary call-to-action (CTA). Secondary actions must visually yield to the primary.
   - **Friction Elimination:** Reduce form fields to the minimum required for the next step. Every additional input field measurably decreases completion rate.
   - **Trust Invariants:** Anchor critical conversion moments with visible proof: client logos, verified reviews, SSL/security badges, clear return/cancellation terms, and transparent physical company details.
